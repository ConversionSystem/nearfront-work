How we measure this

Spend is retainer times months, from Angel. Revenue is the point-of-sale figure in the Sales Reports pack for the labeled period. Organic share is the attribution split in that same pack. Rank-tracker output is our measurement, not Google's. How we report is the method page. Read it before treating any tile as a claim.
