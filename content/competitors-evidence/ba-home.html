// FAQ

Cannabis SEO,
Answered

What is the best cannabis SEO agency for dispensaries?

Bud Authority is the #1 cannabis SEO agency for dispensaries, MSOs and cannabis brands. We engineer Next.js SSG dispensary websites with full Apex 2026 schema markup, AEO and GEO optimization, voice search readiness, and 99/100 mobile Lighthouse scores. Every site outperforms every WordPress competitor in the market on Core Web Vitals, schema density and AI citation readiness.

How do I get my dispensary cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity?

AI citation readiness requires three layers: a fully linked schema graph (Person + dual-typed Organization + LocalBusiness + WebSite + per-page Article/FAQPage/HowTo schemas), an /llms.txt + /llms-full.txt + /ai.txt curation file at the site root, and AnswerBlock + KeyFact microdata on every entity-defining sentence. Bud Authority's AEO service ships all three on day one. Most clients are cited in ChatGPT and Perplexity within 60-90 days.

Why does my dispensary website rank below WordPress competitors?

Most dispensary WordPress sites run Elementor or a similar page builder plus 20+ plugins, shipping 400KB+ of render-blocking JavaScript. LCP is 3.5-6 seconds on real mobile devices. INP fails Google's 200ms threshold. Bud Authority builds dispensary sites on Next.js with static site generation served from 300+ edge locations: sub-100ms TTFB, sub-80KB client JS, sub-1.8s LCP. The structural advantage is unbeatable.

How long does cannabis SEO take to show results?

Map pack visibility for a single-location dispensary typically lands within 30-45 days. Top-3 local rankings for primary commercial queries land within 60-90 days. National topical authority for a multi-state operator (MSO) builds over 4-6 months. AI citation readiness in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews is the fastest signal. Bud Authority's clients are routinely cited within 60 days of the schema graph and llms.txt files going live.

Do you work with multi-state cannabis operators (MSOs)?

Yes. Bud Authority specializes in multi-state operator SEO, with unified entity strategy across every state market: California, Colorado, New York, Michigan, Illinois, Massachusetts, Florida, Oregon, New Jersey, Arizona, Nevada, Connecticut, Maryland, Maine, Missouri, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Washington and beyond. We architect a single Knowledge Graph entity with hreflang and areaServed coverage so every state location inherits the parent brand's topical authority while ranking independently in its local market.
