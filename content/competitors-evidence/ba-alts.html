If you are searching for an alternative to your current cannabis SEO agency, here are 8 real options ranked by client-type fit, with honest pros and cons for each.

Introduction

The reasons cluster into seven patterns. Recognize which one applies to you, because it shapes which alternative is the right move:

Price.

Current agency is over-priced for the deliverable, or under-priced and clearly cutting corners. The "we pay $8,000 a month and the only thing that ships is a thin blog post" pattern is common.

Performance.

Rankings have plateaued or regressed. Google's March 2024 helpful content update and the ongoing April 2026 core update reshaped cannabis SERPs in ways that punished agencies still optimizing for 2019-era playbooks.

Churn risk.

The agency is shrinking, key staff have left, or the relationship feels like it is being managed down. You want to lock in a new partner before the existing one collapses.

Communication.

Account manager rotates every 90 days, response times measured in business days, no proactive insights. The most common churn reason in any SEO retainer category.

Scope creep.

Every request is "out of scope." Adding a new product category requires a change order. Updating five city pages becomes a separate proposal.

No Apex MenuEdge equivalent.

Dutchie products are not in Google's index. Agency has no answer for how to fix it.

No AEO/GEO layer.

Site is not showing up in AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT web search, or Claude citations. Agency does not ship llms.txt, FAQPage at scale, or Speakable schema. The conversation about AI search is met with "that's not a real channel yet."

Section 10

How to Switch Cannabis SEO Agencies Without Losing Rankings

This is the operational question most buyers underestimate. Switching agencies without losing rankings requires six steps in order:

1. Audit the current state.

Pull a full Lighthouse audit, Ahrefs site audit, GSC coverage report, and an inventory of every page indexed today. You need a baseline before you change anything.

2. Document baselines.

Capture current rankings for your top 50 priority keywords, current organic traffic by landing page, current GSC impressions and clicks by query, and current backlink profile. Screenshot everything. The new agency's success measurement depends on a defensible "before" state.

3. Get raw GSC and GA4 access.

Not a screenshot. Not a PDF report. Direct Search Console and GA4 property access in your name, owned by your business email. Many agency relationships go sideways when the agency owns the data property and you cannot transfer it cleanly.

4. Request a 30-day handover.

Ask the outgoing agency for a 30-day overlap where they document everything they ship, hand over schema implementations, content calendars, link-building targets, and any custom code or third-party integrations. Pay for the handover if needed. It is cheaper than reconstructing what they had.

5. Preserve URLs.

Do not let the new agency redesign your URL structure as their first move. URL changes are the single biggest cause of rankings loss in agency transitions. If URLs must change, ensure 301 redirects are mapped one-to-one before launch.

6. Schedule rank tracking from day zero with the new agency.

Use an independent tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Wincher) for rank tracking, not the new agency's internal dashboard. You need a neutral measurement source.

Section 11

What are the best alternatives to my current cannabis SEO agency?

The right alternative depends on your archetype. If you operate a single-store independent dispensary and value operator-voice trust, Deeproots Partners is the alternative. If you operate multiple locations on Dutchie and need product indexation plus AI search citation infrastructure, Bud Authority is the alternative. If you are a cannabis brand needing editorial PR placements, NisonCo is the alternative. If you are leaving Dutchie Plus for native eCommerce, Rank Really High is the alternative. If you are a design-led brand on a higher-DR signal need, CannaPlanners is the alternative.

There is no single "best" alternative, there is the right alternative for your specific business model, current pain point, and competitive set.

Section 12

How do I switch cannabis SEO agencies safely?

Follow the six-step process: audit current state, document baselines (rankings, traffic, GSC impressions, backlinks), get raw GSC + GA4 access in your own name, request a 30-day handover from the outgoing agency, preserve URLs (do not let the new agency redesign URL structure as their first move), and schedule independent rank tracking from day zero. The most common rankings loss during agency switches happens because URLs changed without 301 mapping or because the outgoing agency owned the data properties and the handover was incomplete.
