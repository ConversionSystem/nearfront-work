## Definitions (DefinedTerm set)

- **Cannabis SEO**: search engine optimization tailored to legal cannabis businesses, addressing increased Google scrutiny, manual review policies and limited paid advertising channels.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**: optimizing content and schema so AI search engines cite the page in their answers.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**: optimizing content to influence the training data and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines of large-language models.
- **Zero-Click Search**: search results where the answer appears directly in the SERP (featured snippet, Map Pack, knowledge panel) without a click to the source page.
- **Entity SEO**: building structured-data and same-as relationships so search engines and LLMs recognize a brand as a coherent entity in the Knowledge Graph.
- **Topical Authority**: depth and breadth of content coverage on a topic that signals expertise to both search engines and LLMs.
