Enjoy Clackamas, Oregon

Clackamas, Oregon, is a scenic community just southeast of Portland, known for its lush green spaces and easy access to Mt. Hood. Rooted in agriculture and Native heritage, it’s now a thriving town with outdoor adventure all within reach. 

Wade Creek Vintage Marketplace

This charming vintage store offers a diverse collection of antiques, collectibles, and handcrafted goods. Maybe you're searching for a vintage teapot reminiscent of your grandma's kitchen or a one-of-a-kind piece of wall art; either way, this place has it all.

Skyway Bar & Grill

In Zigzag, Skyway Bar & Grill is renowned for its smoked barbecue, tasty burgers, and a selection of local beers. The rustic ambiance, complemented by live music and outdoor seating, creates a cozy mountain lodge setting that's perfect for unwinding.

The Cazadero Steakhouse

Nearby in Estacada, the Cazadero Steakhouse offers a classic Northwest dining experience with its hearty steaks and panoramic views of the surrounding lake and trees. The restaurant's warm ambiance and attentive service make it a favorite among locals and visitors.

Granny's Drive-Thru

Granny's Drive-Thru in Estacada is a local favorite, serving classic American food like burgers, tots, and chili cheese fries. With its nostalgic drive-thru setup and consistently great food, it’s a popular choice for both quick snacks and relaxed meals.

Old Clackamas Highway Trail

The Old Clackamas Highway Trail traverses green landscapes and offers glimpses of the area's rich history. The trail is a favorite among locals seeking a peaceful outdoor escape. Its gentle terrain and accessibility make it suitable for everyone.

The Old Mill Saloon

The Old Mill Saloon offers classic comfort, cold drinks, and generous portions. Whether you’re craving crispy onion rings, loaded nachos, or a hearty chicken salad, this favorite delivers every time.
