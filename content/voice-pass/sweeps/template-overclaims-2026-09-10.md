# Sitewide sweep: template-level overclaims on the guides (2026-09-10)

The claim classes the Detroit and Michigan fact-checks found were searched for on every production guide (35 pages, base commit 42ff31d), and every hit below was corrected in one production commit. New wording was screened against every sibling guide's 8-word runs before the validator ran. No facts file holds a cross-state comparison, so no national superlative survived.

Kept on purpose, as reasoning rather than sourced fact: stakes lines on the AI-answer cards ("being named is the whole contest", "close to a monopoly"); Nevada's "three candidates statewide" and San Jose's "two candidates in the whole county", which follow from each page's own counts; Baltimore's "a dispensary near me search from Hampden usually finds every candidate a long way off", from the sourced one-per-51,661 density; New York's and Oregon's "search visibility decides who gets found"; Detroit's "every city, village or township that opted out sends its customers somewhere that did not" (owner decision 4). Flagged for sourcing, not changed: Oregon's "roughly four million people", and Portland's "roughly 650,000 people" and Brooklyn's "about four times that population", which are uncited population figures.
