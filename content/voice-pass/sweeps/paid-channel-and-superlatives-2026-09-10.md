# Sitewide sweep 2: paid-channel absolutes, margin claims and superlatives (2026-09-10)

Production d6c614f, on top of the first sweep (816cbd8, `template-overclaims-2026-09-10.md`). The follow-up list from that sweep was Portland's acquisition-cost card, Washington's closure-cause paragraph and FAQ, and the /markets/ hub cards. It grew once the same claim classes were searched with synonyms across every page. The first sweep's search looked for "in the country" and missed "in United States cannabis retail", "Nowhere in America" and "than in almost any other market". It also missed the "no paid channel" family, which our own Weedmaps spoke contradicts, because marketplace placement is a paid channel a dispensary can buy. One of the first sweep's own replacements, Portland's "none of those 231 stores can buy visibility", belonged to that family and is corrected here.

73 replacements on 17 pages and both llms files, plus sitemap lastmod on the four changed pages whose date was older. New wording was screened against every sibling guide's 8-word runs before the validator ran; one collision, Massachusetts against Michigan, was reworded. Visible FAQ answers were checked against their FAQPage copies, and both production validators showed 0 errors.

The rule applied is to say what is sourced. Google and Meta do not allow THC ads (weedmaps-alternative.json wa-no-ads), and Google states there is no way to pay for a better local ranking (wa-organic-costs-nothing). So "no search or social ads" and "the only way onto Google's results" hold. "No paid channel", "cannot buy visibility", "no operator can buy traffic" and "the only acquisition channel available" do not.
