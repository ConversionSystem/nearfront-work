FAQ

Common questions

What does the one service actually include?

Six components, and they are the same six on every engagement: the Google Business Profile, reviews and ratings, map position measured across your trade area, citations and name-address-phone consistency, menu and point-of-sale attribution, and the site and technical work underneath all of it. The explainer sets out what Google documents about each one, and what it does not.

What will you not do?

We will not run ads for THC products. Google's advertising policy disallows ads for substances that alter mental state for recreation, which is the reason the Map Pack matters this much for a dispensary. Past that, the rule is simple: if a piece of work cannot be tied to revenue, calls or conversions, it is not in scope.

How does pricing work?

Scoped per engagement rather than sold as a package, because a single store in a four-dispensary county and a nine-location operator need different amounts of the same work. There is no published rate card. You get a number after we have looked at your market, and the entry point is a free data audit.

How do you measure whether it is working?

Against customer actions rather than screenshots. Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and states on that same page that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking, so a report built only on positions is describing an input you cannot buy. We report profile actions, map position across your trade area, point-of-sale revenue split by new and returning, and search demand by query family.

Do you work with single-location dispensaries, or only multi-location operators?

Both. Single store or nine stores, recreational or medical. The requirement is a dispensary license and local customers to compete for.
