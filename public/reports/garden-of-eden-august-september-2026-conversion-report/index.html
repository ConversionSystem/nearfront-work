01

Summary

From August 1 to September 30 the site took 4,128 tracked online orders worth $287,875, all since tracking began on August 24. Sales per day held flat from late August into September.

Online revenue tracking went live on August 24, so August has just 8 days of sales data (Aug 24 to 31). Over those 8 days the site averaged 111.8 orders and $7,598 a day. September averaged 107.8 orders (-3.5%) and $7,570 (-0.4%) a day, with a slightly larger average order ($70.22 against $68.00).

The biggest win is new order sources. Referral orders per day rose from 1.6 to 5.6, and SMS and Email, which had no orders in the August days, produced 63 orders in September. Organic Search, the largest source, slipped 12% per day and is the one to watch. Google actions from the two profiles were 3.6% lower per day than August, using full months for both.

What happened

Reviews jumped after August 26. Tracy received 24 reviews on August 27 alone and Hayward 14 on August 29, which lines up with review requests starting through the Treez Revenue Kit. August produced 164 new reviews, September 93, and October is running slower at 1.2 a day.

Reply speed improved too. Reviews from late August waited up to about three weeks for a response, while reviews since mid-September are generally answered within a day or two.
