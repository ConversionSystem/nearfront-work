01

Summary

The outcome, in plain terms.

Organic search brought in $76,154 from 846 orders in September, 52% of all online revenue. That is up 134% on September 2025, and organic orders held steady per day versus August (28.2 vs 28.1) while total site traffic dipped after Labor Day.

As of Oct 16, Ember Gardens ranks first on Google for "orleans ma dispensary" in both the map and website results, and has passed Seaside Cannabis in Google reviews, 201 to 197. Google Maps visibility for "cape cod dispensary" nearly doubled, and we published 16 new "dispensary near me" pages on Sep 18.
