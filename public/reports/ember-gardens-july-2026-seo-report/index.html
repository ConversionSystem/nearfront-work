What is Average Ranking Position (ARP)?
ARP is the average position Ember Gardens holds across every point on the map grid for a given search term. Lower is better. An ARP of 1.57 means the store is ranking #1 or #2 at nearly every grid point. An ARP of 3.56 means the store is averaging around position 3 to 4 across the grid, with some gaps where competitors are ahead.
What is Share of Local Voice (SoLV)?
SoLV is the percentage of grid points where Ember Gardens appears in the top 3 results on Google Maps. 100% means the store shows up in the top 3 everywhere across the scan area. 65% means there are roughly 35% of map locations where a competitor is outranking Ember Gardens in the top 3. Higher is better, and 80%+ is strong for a competitive cannabis market.
