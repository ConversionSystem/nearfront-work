1 · Revenue tracking

Revenue tracking was connected. It broke on October 12.

Until Oct 11

The embedded Jane menu sent every order to GA4 with revenue and a unique order ID: purchase, add_to_cart and view_item all firing, one purchase per transaction ID, no duplicates.

Since Oct 12

The site moved to Jane's new hosted menu (checkout now runs on iheartjane.com). Tracked orders fell from 87 a day to 26, add-to-cart events from about 400 a day to 4, and every order since then is credited to Direct or Alpine IQ. Google organic gets credit for zero orders.

Orders tracked in GA4 per day, Sep 15 to Oct 21, 2026

  • Menu pages (/recreational-marijuana-menu/) had 3,630 views on the site Oct 1 to 9. Since Oct 13 they don't appear at all; the menu now opens as a Jane drawer.
  • Sessions on elementalwellnesscenter.com dropped about 40% the same day, because menu browsing no longer happens on the domain.
  • This is almost certainly lost tracking, not lost sales. Treez or Jane order counts for Oct 12 to 21 vs. Oct 1 to 11 will confirm it.

Fix before anything else

  1. Set up GA4 cross-domain tracking between elementalwellnesscenter.com and iheartjane.com, or confirm Jane passes the GA4 client ID into the hosted checkout.
  2. Confirm the new Jane menu sends view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout and purchase to this GA4 property.
  3. Add a fulfillment type (delivery or pickup) to the purchase event so delivery revenue can be measured.
  4. Reconcile one week of GA4 orders against Treez.
2 · Online orders

Online revenue is down 60% year over year

These totals are before the October 12 break, so they reflect real tracked orders. September 2025: $695k from 7,361 orders. September 2026: $281k from 2,656 orders. Average order value rose from $94 to $105, so the drop is fewer orders, not smaller ones.

Online revenue tracked in GA4 by month, Oct 2024 to Sep 2026 (both Septembers highlighted)

Same 90 days, year over year

Sessions −43%, orders −64%, revenue −60%. Conversion rate fell from 16.6% to 10.4% of sessions.

Channel history caveat

From January to August 2025, organic traffic was being counted as Direct (organic fell to about 1,500 sessions a month while Direct jumped). Channel trends before September 2025 are unreliable. Totals are fine.

8 · Tracking cleanup

Smaller GA4 issues to fix in month one

  • Delivery orders can't be separated. "placed_a_delivery_order" fired once in 90 days, and the delivery menu checkout accounts for 215 orders ($31k), about 4% of online revenue, against 30% of total revenue from delivery.
  • staging.elementalwellnesscenter.com and Google Translate traffic are counted in the live property. Add a hostname filter.
  • Non-revenue key events ("about_us", "visited_learn_page") sit next to purchase. Keep purchase and delivery orders as the key events so reports stay clean.
