1Summary

September brought in $184,428 from 3,836 tracked orders, up 6.8% per day over August, plus 3,773 calls, direction requests and website clicks from Google. The biggest win is local visibility. On Google Maps, Buddiez now shows in the top 3 across 76.7% of the Bronx grid for 11 core searches (Oct 16). On the searches tracked in September, share of top 3 rose from 35.8% to 66.3% and the average rank improved from 8.3 to 3.3. "Weed delivery" went from not showing at all to the top 3 on 90% of the grid, and Search Console average position improved from 18.8 to 11.2.
