Broad, across-the-board gains: 10 of the 11 tracked keywords improved Share of Local Voice this scan and none moved backward, pulling the campaign-wide average rank from 5.25 to 4.82 and Share of Local Voice up 17.6 points to 66.6%. The standout is “dispensary fishkill,” whose Share of Local Voice jumped from 2.0% to 100.0%, the strong #1-ish ranking that was concentrated in a narrow slice of the grid last cycle is now showing up across the full grid. “Cannabis dispensary fishkill” (75.5% to 95.9%) and “cannabis store fishkill” (69.4% to 87.8%) posted similarly strong gains, and the broad “near me” terms flagged last cycle, “dispensary open now,” “cannabis near me,” “dispensary near me,” and “cannabis dispensary near me,” all continued improving on both average rank and Share of Local Voice.
🔍
Weakest terms overall: “Weed dispensary near me” remains at 0% SoLV, the same synonym gap flagged for three straight cycles now, likely a content and schema fix rather than a true ranking problem since the “dispensary” and “cannabis” equivalents perform far better. “Dispensary fishkill” is the one term where average rank ticked up slightly, from 1.00 to 1.76, even as its Share of Local Voice rocketed to 100%, the opposite tradeoff from last cycle and a good one to have, wider grid presence at a still-strong average position.
🧠
AI platform visibility (SAIV): “Dispensary hudson valley,” flagged last cycle for Maps and AI platforms moving in opposite directions, resolved that divergence this scan: Share of Local Voice rose from 44.9% to 53.1% and Share of AI Voice nearly doubled from 19.1% to 42.1%, both metrics moving together for the first time in three cycles. “Dispensary near fishkill” now sits at 100% Share of AI Voice, up from 74.0%, and “dispensary near me” jumped from 45.8% to 74.4%. That kind of AI-platform trust building alongside real Maps gains is exactly what a steady flow of fresh Google reviews and the license verification page (section 07) is meant to reinforce.
