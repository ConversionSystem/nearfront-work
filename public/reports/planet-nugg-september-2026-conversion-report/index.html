02

Summary

Revenue held steady and delivery search visibility nearly doubled.

Revenue. Planet Nugg made $1,035,475 from 10,425 online orders in September, 2.2% less per day than August. Organic search, the channel SEO drives, brought in $251,708 from 2,336 orders, with orders up 14.5% on September 2025.

SEO. Google showed planetnugg.com 749,490 times, up 20.7% per day on August. Weed delivery searches by town nearly doubled to 517,152 impressions, and 28 of 70 delivery areas rank in the top 3 on Google.

Work delivered. 529 new backlinks went live from 12 high authority sites (average Domain Rating 90).
