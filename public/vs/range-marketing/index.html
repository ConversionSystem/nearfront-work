The actual difference

How is Nearfront different from Range Marketing?

The difference this page can prove is between named markets and published work. Range's homepage title lists six metros: New York City, Detroit, Phoenix, Las Vegas, Minneapolis and Kansas City. Their own sitemap, checked the same day, contains metro pages for three of them: Phoenix, Kansas City and New York. We found no Detroit, Las Vegas or Minneapolis pages. Naming a market and publishing for it are different commitments, and a buyer in Detroit deserves to know which one they are getting.

Our rule runs the other way: a market appears in our navigation only when its guide exists, is indexable, and every number on it has been re-derived from the state's own file. Eighteen such guides are live. When we could not source a market to a state file, Colorado and New Jersey among them, we did not publish it, and our facts files record why.

On AEO we simply disagree about where the work lives. Range sells it as products. We build it into the pages: sourced Dataset markup, FAQPage on everything, AI crawlers explicitly welcomed, because an answer engine cites the page that can prove its numbers, not the page with the best-named SKU. Reasonable people can prefer either bet; ours is at least inspectable on every page we ship.
