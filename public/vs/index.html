FAQ

Common questions

How do you compare agencies?

Only against what the other agency publishes: its own sitemap, its own pages, its own robots and llms files. Every claim carries the date we checked it. We do not characterize anyone's client work, because that is not visible from outside and we would be guessing.

How often are the comparisons re-checked?

Quarterly. The date on each claim is the claim. A competitor can change their site the day after we look, which is exactly why each page states when we looked instead of asserting a permanent fact.

Why is there no star rating on these pages?

Because that markup would be self-serving. Google scopes aggregateRating to sites that collect reviews about other businesses, which describes directories and review platforms rather than an agency rating its own rivals.

What if something you wrote about my agency is wrong?

Write to bravo@nearfront.com and we will re-verify it against your live site. If we got it wrong, the page is corrected and the correction is dated. We do not quietly edit and move on.

Why these four agencies?

Because each one publishes enough on its own site for a comparison to be checked by a reader. Four is not the whole field, and the page is not a ranking.

Judge us the same way.

Open any market guide, pick a number, and check it against the state file it cites. Then book the free data audit and we will apply that standard to your site.
