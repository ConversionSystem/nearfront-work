# Crawling is ALLOWED here on purpose.
#
# Every response on this host carries "X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow" (see
# public/_headers). That is what actually keeps these pages out of Google.
#
# DO NOT add "Disallow: /". Blocking the crawl PREVENTS Google from ever
# reading the noindex header, and URLs discovered through links then get
# indexed URL-only. That exact failure ("Indexed, though blocked by
# robots.txt") is already an open issue on nearfront.com. Allowing the crawl
# and serving noindex is the combination that works.
#
# There is deliberately no Sitemap: line and no sitemap.xml on this host.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
