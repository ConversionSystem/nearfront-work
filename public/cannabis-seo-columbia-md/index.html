The place that is not a city

Why is Columbia's dispensary count a Howard County number?

Because Columbia is not a city. It is a planned community inside Howard County with no municipal government of its own, and the state's dispensary file records it the way the state sees it: four rows with a Columbia mailing address under the county heading Howard. The Baltimore guide on this site is built around a city that belongs to no county. Columbia is the mirror image, a place everyone treats as a city that belongs entirely to its county.

That changes how you count competitors. The nine Howard County dispensaries in the July 2026 file break down as four in Columbia, two in Elkridge, two in Ellicott City and one in North Laurel. Howard ranks sixth of Maryland's 24 jurisdictions by dispensary count. Someone at Columbia's south end may be as close to the North Laurel store as to any of the four in town.

Distance is measured from the searcher

Who competes for a Columbia dispensary search?

Whoever is closest to the person typing, then whoever is most relevant and prominent among them. Google's own guidance says local results are based primarily on relevance, distance and prominence, and describes distance as how far each result is from the location term in the search or, when there is none, from what Google knows about the searcher's location. That second case is the phone in a parking lot. So the competitive set for a Columbia dispensary search is drawn around the person, not around Columbia, and it moves when they do.

Stand in Town Center and the four 21045 stores are a few minutes east across Route 29, with nothing closer. Stand at the south end of Columbia and the North Laurel store down Route 1 may be as near as any of them. Stand on the northwest edge and Ellicott City's two stores on Baltimore National Pike come into range. Four stores share a mailing city, and no two of them face the same field.

Listing accuracy is part of the same mechanism. Google says it builds a Business Profile from crawled web content, licensed third-party data and user contributions, and that it may update your profile when other sources report your information is incorrect. A store whose directories disagree with its profile on the address or the suite number is inviting exactly that kind of update.
