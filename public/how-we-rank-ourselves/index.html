Two of those lines are what a page like this is for. First, 61 percent of everything Google showed this domain in three months was a legacy URL from the site's previous life: local keyword research tools, franchise marketing, automotive local SEO, pages that still hold positions 13 to 48 for queries we do not serve and now redirect to the nearest live page. Second, the site's best non-brand query is "peptide seo", 322 impressions and 6 clicks at position 9.2, on a vertical we stopped selling on August 25. That page stays live because we decided it would, and it will decay on its own line in this table rather than inside the total.

What the core family shows is a young domain being tested on the right queries and not yet chosen. Google showed this site for "dispensary local seo" 100 times at position 30.0, "cannabis local seo" 95 times at 16.9, "local seo for dispensaries" 74 times at 29.8, "local seo cannabis" 61 times at 12.7 and "cannabis dispensary local seo" 53 times at 29.2, without a click among them. Map Pack intent is there: "how to rank a dispensary on google maps" 22 impressions at 12.3, "dispensary map pack ranking" 14 at 5.1. And the market guides earn on the queries their titles name: "cannabis seo oregon" 19 impressions at 8.9, "dispensary seo colorado" 19 at 17.1, "cannabis seo colorado" 12 at 7.3, "dispensary seo oregon" 10 at 5.6, "los angeles dispensary seo" 8 at 6.4. Seventeen non-brand queries with dispensary or cannabis intent sat in the top ten, every one of them small. Las Vegas drew 15 impressions with no page, and it still gets none, because Nevada publishes no file that isolates the city.
