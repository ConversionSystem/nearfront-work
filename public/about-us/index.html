Our Story

Eighteen years in. We know what works.

Our journey started in 2007, long before local SEO was mainstream. We built one of the first performance-driven SEO agencies, then launched Foottraffik, the first cannabis-focused SEO company, scaling it across dozens of markets before exiting in 2021.

One thing became clear through all of it: businesses in regulated industries needed a system built specifically for them. One that understood compliance restrictions, platform suppression, and how to drive revenue anyway.

Nearfront was built to solve that problem. We operate out of downtown San Diego and serve clients across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, all with the same outcome-first methodology we've refined over nearly two decades.
