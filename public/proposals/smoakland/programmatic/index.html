The honest math on programmatic and new customers

Most media plans lead with the return on ad spend and stop there. We'd rather show you the full picture before you commit budget.

The Honest Math

Programmatic can deliver a real 20x+ return on ad spend. Here's what most agencies won't tell you about where that revenue actually comes from.

20x+
Typical return on ad spend across the programmatic campaigns we've run and reconciled
10–15%
Share of that revenue that's genuinely new customers, even with suppression applied

We reconciled the numbers across every programmatic campaign we've managed or audited, spanning multiple ad platforms and joint commerce data, and the pattern holds nearly every time: suppression lists never match 100% of your existing customer base against the ad platform's identity graph, so some of that "return" is customers who would have bought anyway. That's not a Smoakland problem. It's a structural limit of the channel.

The fix: we upload your customer list securely and suppress it at launch, we monitor match rates through the flight, and we set your expectations up front instead of taking credit for revenue that was already yours.
