Weed delivery: a separate expansion campaign

Zero SoLV on "weed delivery" is not a ranking problem you fix on the existing store pages. It is a content gap. Today the site has one delivery page covering all of NYC with three static borough maps (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens) and no page built to actually rank for a neighborhood or borough search. The blog has local flavor (Downtown Brooklyn record stores, DeKalb Market Hall) but nothing written around delivery intent. This is a build-out, not a tune-up, and it competes for a different kind of search: "weed delivery Park Slope," "weed delivery Astoria," "cannabis delivery Financial District," not "dispensary near me."

The build

One delivery hub, one page per neighborhood, boroughs within reach of your 4 stores first

Same model we use on multi-location delivery buildouts elsewhere: rebuild the delivery hub around an interactive map of your actual delivery area that links out to every neighborhood page, then a dedicated page per neighborhood targeting "weed delivery [neighborhood]" and "cannabis delivery [neighborhood]," starting closest to your 4 stores (Union Square, Flatiron, Gramercy, Chelsea, Nolita, Tribeca, Murray Hill, and Kips Bay in Manhattan; Fort Greene, Boerum Hill, Clinton Hill, and Park Slope in Brooklyn) and expanding into Queens as it proves out.
