The one gap every location shares

Share of Local Voice (SoLV) is the percentage of the grid where a location shows up in the local 3-pack for a given search. Average Rank Position (ARP) is where it lands when it does show up.

Chain-wide finding

Every King of Budz location scores 0% SoLV on every "weed" search we ran

"Weed dispensary," "weed delivery," and "weed delivery near me" all come back at 0% SoLV and an ARP of 21.00 at all 8 locations, which means you are not found at a single one of the 81 grid points. Same story for "marijuana dispensary near me" and "weed dispensary near me" at the three stores where we scanned them. Meanwhile the "dispensary" and "cannabis" phrasings rank fine, up to 86% SoLV in Roseville. Two of your own title tags advertise weed delivery, Inkster and Buffalo, and neither ranks for it anywhere. This isn't one weak store, it's all 8, without exception, and it points to a GBP category/attribute or on-page terminology gap that a single fix can close chain-wide, not eight separate problems.
