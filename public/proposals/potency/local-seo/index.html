Our Recommendation

One location, one clear gap to close

Rename & DBA

File a DBA: “Potency Weed Dispensary Pittsfield MA”

This targets the local gap above directly: Potency ranks 21st or worse, 0% SoLV, on every “weed dispensary” variation (weed dispensary, weed dispensary near me, marijuana dispensary near me), while Canna Provisions owns those exact searches by having put the words “Weed Dispensary” directly in its Google Business Profile name. Where The Content Is Thin

The single biggest opportunity on this page: 10 to 20 new pages of real, human-written content on the pages that already matter. Not spun product blurbs, not AI filler. Written by people who know cannabis retail, sized to actually answer what a shopper is asking. This does two things at once. It gives Google something to rank on pages that currently have almost nothing on them, and it tells the AI platforms, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity, what Potency actually is, what it carries, and who it serves. Those systems increasingly answer “where should I buy weed in Pittsfield” before a shopper ever sees a map. They can only cite a business that has published something worth citing.

Some of the biggest zero-ranking keywords aren’t a link problem at all, they’re a missing-page problem. “Pre rolls” (23,000/mo), “weed cart” (26,000/mo) and “disposable cart” (12,000/mo) already have a matching category page on getpotency.com, and none of them rank, because there isn’t much on the page to rank. Three types of pages are worth building out:

Homepage

Start with the page everything links to

getpotency.com ranks #1 for your own name and almost nothing else, because below the product modules there is very little actual writing. A real content block on the homepage, in your own deli-style voice, linking out to categories, brands and strains, is what passes authority into every page below it and gives the AI systems a single clear statement of who Potency is. We have already written this one, it is ready to publish.

Category Pages

Deepen flower, pre-rolls, edibles & vaporizers

Buying guides, dosing notes and product comparisons on each existing category page give Google, and shoppers, a reason to land there instead of bouncing to a competitor with more to say.

The Fix We’d Start With

Put a prize wheel on the site and turn browsers into a list

You already have the traffic. Right now a first-time visitor can browse the whole menu, leave, and you never learn who they were. A spin-to-win wheel with eight offers on it fixes that, and the sequence matters as much as the wheel does. We run this exact funnel for another cannabis retailer and it converts well above a plain newsletter box, because the visitor is working toward a prize rather than being asked for a favor.

Spin To Win · Lead Capture Modal

The live version running for another cannabis retailer we work with. Note what it asks for and what it does not: two fields, a visible prize, and a clear way out for anyone who just wants to browse. The phone number comes later, once they have won something.

Step 1

Name and email, before the spin

Two fields, nothing else. The ask is small and the payoff is immediate, so it clears the bar that a standard signup form never does. The lead is captured whether or not they ever use the coupon.

Step 2

Eight offers on the wheel

Dollar-off, percent-off and product-specific prizes, weighted the way you want them weighted. Eight segments is enough to feel like real odds, and every prize is one you were willing to give anyway.

Step 3

Phone number to claim it

They have already won at this point, so asking for a number and a marketing opt-in to deliver the code converts far better than asking cold. That is how the SMS list gets built, with consent, one prize at a time.

It ties into Dutchie, so the codes are real ones that redeem at checkout and every lead lands in the same customer record you already sell to. That gives the email and SMS programs an actual list to work with, and it makes the review automation above worth more too, since more first-time buyers means more requests going out. Same integration, two jobs.
