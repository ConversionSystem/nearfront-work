The one gap every location shares

Share of Local Voice (SoLV) is the percentage of the grid where a location shows up in the local 3-pack for a given search. Average Rank Position (ARP) is where it lands when it does show up.

Chain-wide finding

Every single BUZZ location scores 0% SoLV on "weed dispensary"

This isn't one weak store. It's all 8, without exception, and it holds true even at Santee and Sorrento Valley, the two locations that otherwise dominate their own grids on every other term. ARP 21.00 means not found at a single one of the 81 grid points, not merely ranking poorly. "Dispensary" and "dispensary near me" both rank normally at the same locations and the same grids, so this isn't a market-strength problem, it's a GBP category, attribute, or on-page terminology gap that a single fix can close chain-wide, not eight separate problems.
