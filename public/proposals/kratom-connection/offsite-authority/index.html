Why an authority asset, not a link list

Kratom is one of the hardest link-building categories there is.

Two things are true at once: the long tail of kratom queries is nearly uncontested, with Ahrefs keyword difficulty near zero, yet the site ranks page 3 to 6 for them on weak on-page and internal linking. The real money terms ("kratom capsules," "buy kratom," "buy kratom online") sit behind competitors with two to three times the domain authority, winnable only through a long, expensive guest-post game in a category most publishers won't touch, and that path hands ranking control to third-party webmasters who can delete a link anytime.
