SEO growth, phased across 12 months
Phase 1 · Now

SEO: foundation

Fix the GBP delivery category gap on both listings, rebuild the delivery landing page, bring Tracy's on-site content and links up to Hayward's standard, and launch geo-expansion pages for Sunol, Livermore, and Mountain House, backed by a structured authority and AI-visibility program (interlinked social profiles, tiered drip-feed indexing, authority hubs with citations, and roughly 20 contextual, high-authority backlinks to the homepage first) that is set up in month 1 and starts active spend in month 2, once the delivery-zone pages exist to support it; the first three months focus on overall site authority and closing the weed delivery visibility gap, and this proposal is SEO-focused, with programmatic retargeting available separately as an optional add-on, not part of either core package.

Phase 2 · Month 4–6

SEO: expansion

Geo-expansion pages extend to the next tier of delivery cities. The backlink program moves past the homepage to the delivery hub page and any city page outside the top 5. Weed delivery visibility and Domain Rating climb toward closing the gap with Cookies and Stiiizy, and AI surfacing improves alongside it. If the retargeting add-on is active, it continues at the same lean budget, reinvesting returns rather than scaling media spend.

Phase 3 · Month 7–12

SEO: maturity

Full city-page coverage across both delivery zones, Domain Rating matured off a year of contextual link building, and organic search carrying the majority of incremental revenue growth. If active, the retargeting add-on is reviewed against a full year of data and rebudgeted only if the existing-customer list has grown enough to justify it.

This proposal reflects our full engagement tier: Garden of Eden has granted Google tools access plus point-of-sale and loyalty system access, which is what makes a model like this possible in the first place. This plan intentionally excludes paid Google Ads and Meta acquisition spend and is SEO-focused: the two package options above cover the authority and backlink program only. Programmatic retargeting is available as a separate, optional add-on for accounts that want a lean, existing-customer paid channel running alongside SEO, but it is not required to hit either package's revenue targets modeled here. The revenue forecast above is tabbed to match: Package 1 (core) and Package 2 (full) each show their own monthly projection, since the two packages invest in different scopes of backlink work and shouldn't share one growth curve.
