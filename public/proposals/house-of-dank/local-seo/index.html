The one gap every location shares

Share of Local Voice (SoLV) is the percentage of the grid where a location shows up in the local 3-pack for a given search. Average Rank Position (ARP) is where it lands when it does show up.

Chain-wide finding

Every single House of Dank location scores 0% SoLV on "weed delivery" and "weed delivery near me"

This isn't one weak store. It's all 16, without exception, and not on one term but two. Zero of 81 grid points at every location, statewide. Nine of your sixteen Google Business Profiles already carry a "Delivery service" category and the site advertises delivery hours at ten locations, so the intent is filed but Google isn't surfacing it. Meanwhile "weed dispensary near me" shows the same pattern while "dispensary near me" performs well, which points at a naming, category and on-page terminology gap around the word "weed" that a single fix can close chain-wide, not sixteen separate problems.
